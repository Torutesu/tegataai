    Definitions & assumptions

    • provider_cost — sum of provider spend attributed to the user in the window.
    • recognized_revenue — subscriptions prorated daily across their period; one-time payments recognized straight-line over the 30 days after the charge (an approximation of consumption-based recognition — the export cannot show consumption); promotional grants: 0.
    • gross_margin = recognized_revenue − provider_cost.
    • negative_margin_user — gross_margin < 0 over the selected rolling window.
    • The window ends at the newest date found in your files, not at today.
    • Proration is in effect: no per-user key could be matched, so total provider cost was distributed across paying users in proportion to recognized revenue. Every figure on this page is an estimate.
