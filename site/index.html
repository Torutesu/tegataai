Subscriptions sell. Variable cost decides whether they were worth selling.

AI apps convert trials 52% better than non-AI apps — and churn out of annual plans about 30% faster. All-you-can-eat pricing breaks against a bill that moves per request, and only about one app in ten runs a working hybrid of subscription and credits.

Existing billing tells you which users cost you money. It tells you after the month closed, from the provider's invoice.

An authorization, not an invoice

Before each action, one call: allowed, refused, or "offer the cheaper model." A refusal is not an error — it returns the reason and the fix, so the moment a user hits the wall becomes the moment they top up.

Held, then settled

A face value is reserved before the call and settled against what was actually used. The remainder returns on its own.

The refusal is data

Balance low, balance gone, margin turned negative — each fires an event with a holdout group attached, so you see whether the intervention worked, not whether it ran.

Cheaper, or nothing

A fallback is proposed only when it is genuinely cheaper and genuinely affordable. We never route your request ourselves.

Your prompts never pass through us — the SDK sends token counts, not content. If we are unreachable, your app keeps running: the degraded posture is a setting you choose in advance, not an outage you discover.

Export the register. Verify it yourself.

Every hold, every settlement, every refusal lands in one append-only register. The hash chain is defined over the entry itself, so anyone holding an export can check the arithmetic without trusting the service that produced it.

$ curl /v1/register/export | tegata-verify
  user_8f21   balance 991   reserved 0   available 991
  chain verifies — 5 entries across 2 subjects, manifest agrees

$ # alter one byte
  FAIL  user_8f21 seq 2: hash does not match the entry contents
  chain FAILED — 1 issue(s)

TEGATA

A tegata was a written promise to pay, used in Japanese commerce for centuries: a face value, a maturity, a drawer, and a chain of endorsements. The paper is ending. The structure is not.

Billing systems tell you what happened. TEGATA decides what's allowed to happen.
